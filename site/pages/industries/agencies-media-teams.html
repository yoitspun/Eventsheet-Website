Why Agencies & Media Teams Love Eventsheet

While other teams drown in repetitive setup work, agencies that use Eventsheet can scale their book of business without burning out their staff or inflating overhead.

95%+ Faster Campaign Deployment

Turn media plans into live campaigns in a fraction of the time. Upload a sheet, map your variables, and ship campaigns across multiple ad accounts and platforms while your team moves on to the next brief.

Standardized Execution Across Clients

Replace one-off workflows with consistent, reusable templates. Ensure every launch follows the same naming conventions, structure, and best practices, so your team stays organized and your reporting stays clean.

More Testing Without More Work

Spin up more creative and audience variations without increasing manual effort. Use variables for headlines, angles, and offers to quickly produce, test, and learn across multiple clients and markets.

Multi-Platform From One Workflow

Support Meta now, with Google, Reddit, and LinkedIn coming soon, all from a single workflow. Avoid jumping between interfaces and give your team a unified way to execute campaigns for every client.

Client-Ready Visibility

Quickly understand what is running where so you can respond fast to client questions. View performance by client, account, or campaign type and use those insights to improve strategy and retention.
